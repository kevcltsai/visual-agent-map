Tone: narrative. Write for readers who want to keep reading: keep the section headings, but write each section as connected prose instead of a bullet list; carry each section's main point with one concrete example or scene, and let sentences follow on from one another naturally. Sections that are a table or a to-do list by design (such as an evidence table or steps) keep that form. Keep the content accurate: no exaggeration and no invention; say plainly what is uncertain.
