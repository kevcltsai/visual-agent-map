Additional requirements of the skim list format (they follow the lean format above):

- Let the reader catch the point at a glance: each point says one thing, in one sentence, states the conclusion directly, and has no build-up or transition sentences.
- Use a bullet list for whatever can be listed; keep each item to two sentences at most, put numbers and names first, and do not pile up adjectives.
- A plain paragraph is two sentences at most; two or more options or comparison dimensions still go in a Markdown table.
- This only limits the writing: the element limits and safety rules of the lean format stay, so rules 1 to 7 stay as they are and callouts, diagrams, highlight marks and task lists are still not allowed.
