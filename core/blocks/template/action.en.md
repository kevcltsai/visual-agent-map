## Goal
What this action should achieve, stated in a measurable way.

## Steps
List what to do in order, one step per line, as a numbered list.

## Checkpoints
The times or indicators used along the way to confirm the direction is right.

## Done when
What counts as finished; write conditions that can be checked.

## Evidence and sources
The facts and data that support the conclusions, with their sources; the source list is added to the end of this section automatically.

## Update log
Add only one line saying what this update changed.
