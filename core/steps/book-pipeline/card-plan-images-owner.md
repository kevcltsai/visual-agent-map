- images: the picture plan for this chapter. The owner set the number of pictures per chapter in the whole-book settings: give {{count}} for this chapter, whatever its role (an introduction, a closing or a gallery chapter too). Plan fewer than {{min}} only when the chapter truly has nothing to picture. Spread them over the points the chapter makes, one picture per point, each beside something the chapter's text talks about. Each slot has: kind (the kind of picture, for example painting, photograph, map, diagram, portrait; a few words), explains (what the picture shows or explains, one sentence that names something the chapter's text talks about), anchor (where it sits: the paragraph or point in the chapter it goes beside) and search (2 to 4 English search terms for a Wikimedia Commons file search, a few words each, most specific first: the work's or object's usual English name, the artist or person, the place; always in English, whatever the book's language). Do not number the slots. Do not write image links or addresses; a picture is found later.
